Today I split my time between client projects and coursework, which keeps both sides sharp: client work keeps my code practical, and CSE keeps my fundamentals solid.

I specialize in WordPress custom themes, WooCommerce stores, and Elementor builds but I also hand-code when a client needs something a template can't do.
